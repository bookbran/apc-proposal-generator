4. Billing & Invoicing

Client will be invoiced [Total per Employee] per employee upon completion of that employee's initial strategy session with Dan Hahn, M.S.   • Intake Deadline: Each eligible employee must reach out and complete their initial intake session within [Intake Deadline Days] days of their separation date to be eligible for services under this Agreement.
  • Usage Window: Each participant has up to [Usage Window Months] months from the date of their intake session to utilize their [Hours per Employee] hours of coaching.

6. Unused Hours & Refund Policy

If a participant does not use all [Hours per Employee] hours within the [Usage Window Months] month window, Provider will issue a prorated refund to Client for the unused portion. The refund will be calculated at the per-hour rate of [Rate per Hour]. [Include example calculation here.]

7. Reporting

Provider will deliver monthly usage reports to Client showing each participant's progress, including hours used, hours remaining, and general engagement status. These reports ensure Client has full visibility into how the program is being utilized.

8. Confidentiality

Provider will maintain the confidentiality of all participant coaching sessions. Usage reports shared with Client will include hours and engagement status only; the specific content of coaching conversations will remain confidential between the participant and their assigned coach.

9. Term & Termination

This Agreement becomes effective upon execution by both Parties and remains in effect until all participant engagements have concluded or all usage windows have expired, whichever comes first. Either Party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice. In the event of early termination, Client will be responsible only for services already rendered, and Provider will issue a prorated refund for any unused, pre-invoiced hours.
