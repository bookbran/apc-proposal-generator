Career Transition Services Agreement  |  Lewis & Clark College
February 2026

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date signed below, by and between A Portland Career, a career coaching and consulting firm based in Portland, Oregon ("Provider"), and Lewis & Clark College, located in Portland, Oregon ("Client"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."

1. Scope of Services

Provider agrees to deliver personalized career transition coaching services to up to fifteen (15) departing staff members designated by Client. Each participant will receive individualized support designed to accelerate their career transition and next-opportunity readiness.

2. Services Included

Each participant's engagement includes the following:

Initial 1-on-1 strategy session with Dan Hahn, M.S.
Personalized coach matching based on goals
Resume & cover letter development
LinkedIn profile optimization
Interview preparation & coaching
Job search strategy & networking guidance
Career planning & skills assessment
Industry landscape navigation

3. Engagement Terms

Hours per Participant 15 Hours Individual cap, no pooling across cohort
Rate $180 / hour $2,700 per employee
Maximum Participants 15 Employees Departing Lewis & Clark staff
Maximum Investment $40,500 Only for employees who actively engage

Each participant receives a dedicated fifteen (15) hour individual allocation. Hours are not pooled across the cohort and may not be redistributed between participants. This structure ensures equity and predictability on a per-person basis.

4. Billing & Invoicing

Client will be invoiced $2,700 per employee upon completion of that employee's initial strategy session with Dan Hahn, M.S. Payment is due within fourteen (14) days of invoice date. Client is only billed for employees who actively engage in the program. If a designated employee does not schedule or complete an intake session, no charge will be incurred for that individual.

Payment may be made by ACH bank transfer (preferred) or check. ACH payment instructions will be included on each invoice. Invoices unpaid after thirty (30) days may be subject to a late fee of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

5. Eligibility & Timeline

  • Intake Deadline: Each eligible employee must reach out and complete their initial intake session within sixty (60) days of their separation date to be eligible for services under this Agreement.
  • Usage Window: Each participant has up to nine (9) months from the date of their intake session to utilize their fifteen (15) hours of coaching.

6. Unused Hours & Refund Policy

If a participant does not use all fifteen (15) hours within the nine (9) month window, Provider will issue a prorated refund to Client for the unused portion. The refund will be calculated at the per-hour rate of $180. For example, if a participant uses ten (10) hours, Client will receive a refund of $900 for the five (5) unused hours.

7. Reporting

Provider will deliver monthly usage reports to Client showing each participant's progress, including hours used, hours remaining, and general engagement status. These reports ensure Client has full visibility into how the program is being utilized.

8. Confidentiality

Provider will maintain the confidentiality of all participant coaching sessions. Usage reports shared with Client will include hours and engagement status only; the specific content of coaching conversations will remain confidential between the participant and their assigned coach.

9. Term & Termination

This Agreement becomes effective upon execution by both Parties and remains in effect until all participant engagements have concluded or all usage windows have expired, whichever comes first. Either Party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice. In the event of early termination, Client will be responsible only for services already rendered, and Provider will issue a prorated refund for any unused, pre-invoiced hours.

By signing below, both Parties agree to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.
