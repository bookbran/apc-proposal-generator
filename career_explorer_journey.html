Your Career Exploration Journey
A guided process to discover what's next — and build a plan that's truly yours
Career Explorer Track  ·  7 Sessions
This map shows the full arc of our work together. Each phase builds on the last — from getting to know yourself deeply, to exploring the world of possibilities with your coach, to landing on a clear and energizing plan for what comes next. You don't need all the answers going in. That's what this process is for.
1
Self-Discovery Sessions 1–3  ·  Who you are, what energizes you
Session 1
Story & Interests
  • Share your life story and career history
  • Explore interests across the decades
  • Identify themes that energize you
  • Begin building rapport & psychological safety
Between Sessions
Complete IPIP 300 & 16 Personalities assessments
Session 2
Personality & Self-Discovery
  • Debrief personality assessment results together
  • Explore how traits show up in past work
  • Identify environments where you thrive
  • Begin connecting insights to career directions
Between Sessions
Complete Motivated Skills Matrix & Gallup Strengths
Session 3
Strengths & Career Preferences Model
  • Review strengths assessment together
  • Build your Career Preferences Model (6–8 criteria)
  • Connect strengths to what matters most
  • Discuss with someone who knows you well
Between Sessions
Refine your preferences model; discuss top criteria with a trusted person
2
Guided Mapping Sessions 4–6  ·  Exploring the world of possibilities together ✦ Coach & client work side-by-side in real time
Session 4
Industry Discovery
  • Finalize your Career Preferences Model together
  • Co-explore industries using AI, O*NET & CareerExplorer
  • React in real time — your coach watches for energy
  • Surface 3–4 industries worth investigating further
Between Sessions
Read one article on each of two industries that felt most alive
Session 5
Organization & Role Exploration
  • Co-browse target companies and LinkedIn profiles
  • Explore role types and job postings together
  • Build your organization and role list in real time
  • Notice what pulls your attention and curiosity
Between Sessions
Reach out to one person you know adjacent to a field of interest — a curiosity conversation, not a job ask
Session 6
Networking for Exploration
  • Debrief your first curiosity conversation
  • Prepare outreach messages for 2–3 informational interviews
  • Select questions to ask; role-play the conversation
  • Learn what you need to confirm interest & direction
Between Sessions
Send outreach messages drafted in session; conduct informational conversations
3
Pathway Planning Session 7  ·  Your plan forward
Session 7
Synthesis & Transition Roadmap
  • Review all you've learned and explored
  • Use your preferences model as a decision filter
  • Co-create your transition roadmap — job search, skill-building, education, volunteering, or more exploration
  • Leave with clarity, direction, and a clear next step
This journey is yours. Your coach walks with you every step of the way — you are never doing this alone.
